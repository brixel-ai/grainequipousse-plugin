# Bringing a finished post

graine qui pousse serves this skill, always in step with its tools: call `read_skill("bringing-a-finished-post")` and follow what it says. Its supporting files (references, worked examples) come the same way, `read_skill("bringing-a-finished-post", file=…)`: the skill names them.
