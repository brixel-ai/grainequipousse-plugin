# Building a dossier

This skill lives on graine qui pousse's server, always in step with its tools: call `read_skill("building-a-dossier")` and follow what it says. Its other files (references, worked examples) come the same way, `read_skill("building-a-dossier", file="…")`, as the skill names them. A text sent in parts says on its first line how to read the next: keep the same arguments.

No `read_skill` tool in your list? graine's connector is not connected: ask the user to connect it (`/mcp` in Claude Code, the plugin's Connectors tab in Claude).
