# Directing a shoot

graine qui pousse serves this skill, always in step with its tools: call `read_skill("directing-a-shoot")` and follow what it says — with `post_id="…"` when you work on a post: it opens with that post's frame in px, the user's brief, the design level, the clock and the worked examples that fit. Its supporting files (references, worked examples) come the same way, `read_skill("directing-a-shoot", file=…)`: the skill names them.
